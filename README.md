# 👑 Police & Thief

### _Pick your fate. Find the thief._

A beautiful online version of the classic Kerala paper game **Police & Thief** — rebuilt for friends to play together from their phones.

Create a room. Invite your friends. Pick a secret slot. Discover your role.  
And if you're the Police... **find the Thief.** 🕵️

## 🎭 What is Police & Thief?

Remember those childhood paper games where everyone secretly picked a folded paper and got a role?

**King. Queen. Police. Thief.**

Police & Thief brings that same feeling to the screen.

No complicated setup.  
No accounts.  
No boring menus.

Just create a room, send the code to your friends, and start playing.

---

## 🕵️ How It Works

### 01 — Create a Room

One player creates a room and chooses:

- 👥 Number of players
- 🔄 Number of rounds
- 👑 Role points
- 🎭 Additional roles
- ⚙️ Game preferences

Then share the room code with your friends.

---

### 02 — Join Your Friends

Enter the room code and choose your name.

You'll enter the lobby and wait for everyone to arrive.
